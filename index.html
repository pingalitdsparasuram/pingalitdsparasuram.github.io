Burp & Beep Autopilot

Burp & Beep Autopilot is a private tool used only by the owner of the Burp & Beep YouTube channel, a wordless animated comedy channel.

It runs on the channel owner's own computer and uses the official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload and schedule the channel's own videos with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails;
  • read the channel's own video statistics to find the best posting time;
  • update the titles of the channel's own videos.

It is not offered to the public and does not access any other channel or account.
